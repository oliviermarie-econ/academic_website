Welcome to my website!

I am Professor of Labour Economics at the Erasmus School of Economics in Rotterdam, where I am also Research Director of the Health, Labour and Inequalities Group (HELIq).

I have research affiliations at the Tinbergen Institute, IZA, CEPR, and CESifo.

My main research interests include: economics of crime, labour economics, economics of education, family economics, micro-econometrics, and public economics.
